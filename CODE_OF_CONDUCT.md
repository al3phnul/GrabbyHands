# Code of Conduct

GrabbyHands steals pixels, not people's dignity. Here's the deal.

## The short version

Be decent. Roast the code, not the people. Snark about the software is
welcome; snark aimed at humans isn't. If you can't manage that, your
comments get deleted and you get shown the door.

## Don't

- **Be a dick**: in issues, PRs, discussions, or anywhere you're
  representing the project.
- **Be creepy.** This is a screenshot tool, not a dating app. Don't be weird.
- **Be a bigot.** Nobody has to put up with shit from people simply due to
  their race, gender, sexual orientation, religion (or lack thereof),
  disability, age, or anything else about who they are. If you want to see
  me get real petty, break this rule.
- **Troll.** Bug reports written as insults get closed as
  "Won't fix: Not my job to raise you right."

## Do

- Be Not A Dick.
- Assume good intent until proven otherwise.
- Give feedback that actually helps someone fix something.
- Remember the maintainer is one guy doing this for free.

## How Community Moderation Works

I'm the maintainer, judge, jury, and wielder of the ban-hammer. These are
things I may recruit help with if the community grows. But while it's just
me, know that I tend to match people's energy. Be cool, and you'll be
treated cool. Be a dick, and I will likely be reciprocally phallic.

## Reporting

Email **[grabbyhands.bulb292@passinbox.com](mailto:grabbyhands.bulb292@passinbox.com)**.
Reports stay confidential. Include links or screenshots. You've got a pretty
good tool for that.

## Scope

This applies to the GrabbyHands repo and anywhere else the project hangs out
(Discord, socials, etc.).
